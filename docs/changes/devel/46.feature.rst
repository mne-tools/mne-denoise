Added ``mne_denoise.sound.SOUND`` and ``mne_denoise.sspsir.SSPSIR``, ports of
two forward-model denoisers from the TMS-EEG literature. SOUND (Mutanen et al.,
2018) estimates each sensor's noise amplitude by predicting it from the others
through a forward model, then Wiener-filters to suppress channel-specific noise
while preserving field-consistent brain signal. SSP-SIR (Mutanen et al., 2016)
projects out the high-variance muscle-artifact subspace and reconstructs what
that projection removed through the same model, crossfading it in only where
the artifact lives, and exposes the deleted topographies as ``projs_``. Both
follow the sklearn-style ``fit``/``transform`` API, take MNE ``Raw``,
``Epochs``, ``Evoked`` objects or arrays and an optional ``mne.Forward``, and
otherwise build a three-layer spherical lead field from the montage. SOUND's
noise update uses the covariance identity of Mutanen et al. (2022),
Eqs. (35)-(36), touching the time samples once instead of inside every
iteration, so runtime is effectively independent of recording length. Also
added ``mne_denoise.quantify_overcorrection``, which measures how much any
linear spatial filter in this package attenuates or distorts hypothetical
cortical sources by pushing its operator through the forward model. Both
estimators ship with gallery examples and are checked against Tuomas Mutanen's
``Sound-Demo-Package`` and EEGLAB's TESA (Issue #32, PR #46).
