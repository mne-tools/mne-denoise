Added the ``mne_denoise.asr`` module for Artifact Subspace Reconstruction. It
provides a standard ASR estimator with array-level calibration/processing
helpers, clean_rawdata-compatible clean-RMS distribution fitting, ASR-specific
QA metrics and diagnostics, and MATLAB-backed parity fixtures. The module also
exposes MATLAB-reference-backed ``AdaptiveASR`` (``psp`` / ``psw`` / ``mw``)
variants with a scikit-learn-style ``fit`` / ``partial_fit`` / ``transform``
API, and source-backed ``JugglerASR`` calibration variants implementing the
sample-wise ASRDBSCAN and ASRGEV reference-selection procedures from Kim et al.
(2025), plus the ``mne_denoise.asr.select_juggler_reference_samples`` helper,
tests, docs, and synthetic gallery examples. Validation tooling under
``scripts/`` injects known burst artifacts on real EEG and reproduces headline
figures from Chang et al. (2020), Blum et al. (2019), and Kim et al. (2025). Calibration and
standard/adaptive processing honor an optional ``max_mem_mb`` budget through
memory-bounded covariance aggregation, preserving the full-memory path for
small datasets.
